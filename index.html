About

I am a PhD student at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University, advised by Prof. Yangfan Zhou.

My research focuses on software engineering for mobile applications, particularly automated GUI testing and LLM-driven testing agents.

SwipeGen: Bridging the Execution Gap in GUI Agents via Human-like Swipe Synthesis

Xuan Wang*, Siyuan Su*, Quantong Fu*, Yongxiang Hu, Yangfan Zhou

Proceedings of the 34th ACM International Conference on Multimedia (MM'26)

SwipeGen synthesizes diverse, human-like swipe interactions and improves GUI agents' ability to execute realistic mobile gestures.

From Redundancy to Efficiency: Exploiting Shared UI Interactions towards Efficient LLM-Based Testing
